# QuoteRight — Product brief
**Complex quotes. Done right.**

**Category:** AI-native quoting and proposal automation; deterministic CPQ underneath.

**Customer problem:** B2B sellers and solution engineers spend substantial time turning unstructured customer requirements into valid configurations, prices and proposals, coordinating across product, finance and approvals. Manual work increases turnaround time and rework.

**Value proposition:** Transform customer requirements into accurate, policy-compliant, approval-ready quotes with less manual effort. AI handles ambiguity and workflow coordination; deterministic systems ensure commercial correctness.

**ICP:** B2B companies selling configurable products/services with nontrivial pricing and approval requirements. Suitable for SMB-to-enterprise architecture, starting with a simple fictional SaaS seller for the prototype.

**Primary users:** Account executives, sales engineers; secondary: deal desk / RevOps, approvers.

**Workflow:** Intake requirements → clarify missing details → propose configuration → validate products → calculate authoritative price → flag/route exceptions → assemble quote/proposal → human review → revise as necessary.

**North-star business goal:** Reduce time from requirements to approval-ready quote while maintaining correctness.

**Measures:** Preparation time, configuration validity, exact pricing reconciliation, requirement coverage, quote rework, approval compliance.

**Safety boundary:** AI never independently determines authoritative commercial prices or bypasses approvals.
